Legal

Privacy policy.

Last updated July 2026.

What is collected. When you create an account, the email address and name you provide are stored by Supabase Authentication. When you submit an order, the details you enter (name, email, company, phone, project information, and any file you attach) are stored in a Supabase (Postgres) database.

How it's used. Order details are used only to scope, price, and deliver your project, and to follow up with you by email. Account information is used to let you sign in and to associate your orders with your account.

Who can see it. Only you and the studio owner (via the admin dashboard) can read your order details — Firestore security rules prevent any other visitor from reading, editing, or deleting order data.

Third parties. Data is hosted on Supabase (Authentication, Postgres database, and Storage). No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Your choices. You can request deletion of your account and associated orders at any time by emailing devora.inbox@gmail.com.

This is a template policy for a small freelance studio and isn't a substitute for formal legal advice — update it to reflect your actual practices before relying on it.
